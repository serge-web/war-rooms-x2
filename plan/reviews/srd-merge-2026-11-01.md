# SRD merge: the product owner's revision, Nov 1, 2026

The product owner's working copy of the SRD (with new Annexes A and B) merged into `WRX1-SRD.md`. Internal record; not published on the site.

**Inputs.** The repo SRD at commit 275ed4f, and the owner's working copy. A line diff shows the owner's copy is the repo text at commit 9c3b7b9 (the "Oct 20" text, before the Oct 24 revision notes) plus eleven edited lines and the two annexes. So every other difference between the two documents is a repo decision made since then, which the owner's copy did not yet have.

**Rules applied** (from the owner, Nov 1):

1. Merge into the repo SRD: keep the repo's decisions since Oct 20, and apply the owner's changes on top.
2. Objectives: only the admin edits them; the White Force (observers too) reads every force's objectives.
3. Entity ids become GUIDs now (Phase 3), with a separate readable login name, and a last-name record for deleted forces and roles.

Anything else where the two texts conflict on a product decision is kept as the repo has it and listed first, below.

## Every semantic difference

"Applied" means the owner's text is in the merged SRD (sometimes reworded to fit the repo's later decisions, as noted). "Kept repo" means the repo text stays because the owner's copy carries the older Oct 20 text, not a new decision. "Derived" means a change the owner's decisions require elsewhere in the SRD, which neither text had.
